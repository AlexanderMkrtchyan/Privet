Why Privet

Built for conversations that feel instant.

Everything you expect from a modern messenger, without the bloat.

Real-time & fast

Messages, typing indicators, and presence delivered live over WebSockets. No refresh, no waiting.

Voice & video calls

Crystal-clear WebRTC calls and screen sharing, built right into every conversation.

Everywhere you are

Native desktop clients for Windows and Linux, plus a full-featured web app. Your chats follow you.

Private by default

Your account, your data. Token-based auth and a self-hosted backend you control.

Media & files

Share images, voice notes, and files with rich previews and instant playback.

Light & focused

A calm, distraction-free interface that loads in a blink and stays out of your way.
